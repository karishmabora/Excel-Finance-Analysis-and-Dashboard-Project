# Excel-Finance-Analysis-and-Dashboard-Project
## Finance Dashboard – Excel Project

An interactive Excel finance dashboard built to analyze and visualize sales performance from raw transactional data, transforming it into clear, decision-ready insights.

## 🔑 Highlights
KPI Summary Cards – Total Sales (₹4,38,968), Average Sales per Day (₹553), Number of Products (6), and Rounded Sales Variance (₹103) for a quick performance snapshot.
Interactive Slicers – Filter the entire dashboard by Weekday and Product ID for dynamic, on-demand analysis.
Daily Sales Trend – Combo chart comparing total sales vs. average sales across weekdays to spot patterns and outliers.
Actual vs Rounded Sales – Bar chart comparing raw sales figures against sales rounded to the nearest ₹5, segmented by price bucket.
Sales by Price Bucket – Breakdown of total sales value across four price ranges (Below 300, 300–500, 500–700, 700 Above).
Product Volume Analysis – Units sold per price bucket to understand which price ranges drive the highest transaction volume.
Product-wise Performance – Horizontal bar and line combo comparing total sales value against average price per Product ID (PIZB0001–PIZB0006).

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques
Microsoft Excel (PivotTables & PivotCharts)
Slicers for interactive filtering
Formulas for KPI calculations (SUM, AVERAGE, ROUND, DISTINCT COUNT)
Dashboard design principles (layout, color coding, readability)

This project demonstrates my ability to turn raw data into a polished, interactive reporting tool — a skill directly applicable to business analytics, financial reporting, and data-driven decision-making.
